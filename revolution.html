A Movement for Freedom

The Philippine Revolution began in 1896 as Filipinos organized to end Spanish colonial rule. The Katipunan, founded in 1892, played a central role in the movement.

The revolution involved many communities and leaders. Revolutionary forces also faced military pressure, limited resources, and disagreements among leaders.

Important Ideas

Organization

The Katipunan helped organize people who wanted independence from Spain.

Participation

People contributed through organizing, communication, supplying resources, and resistance.

Challenges

Revolutionary groups faced military pressure and disagreements among their leaders.

Legacy

The revolution became an important part of the continuing struggle for self-government.
