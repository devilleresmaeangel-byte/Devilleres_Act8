Places Can Tell Stories

Historical landmarks connect us to important events, people, architecture, and traditions.

Fort Santiago

Manila

A historic fort in Intramuros associated with Spanish colonial Manila. José Rizal was imprisoned here before his execution.

Aguinaldo Shrine

Kawit, Cavite

The former home of Emilio Aguinaldo, associated with the proclamation of Philippine independence on June 12, 1898.

Barasoain Church

Malolos, Bulacan

This church is connected with the Malolos Congress and the establishment of the First Philippine Republic in 1899.

Mactan Shrine

Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu

A memorial area associated with the 1521 Battle of Mactan and the resistance led by Lapulapu against Magellan's expedition.

Be a Responsible Visitor

Follow site rules, respect memorial spaces, avoid damaging historic structures, and learn from museum displays and guides.
