June 12, 1898

Emilio Aguinaldo proclaimed Philippine independence from Spain in Kawit, Cavite. The event included the public reading of the Act of the Declaration of Independence and the public display of the Philippine flag.

The struggle for recognized independence continued after the declaration. The United States recognized the independence of the Republic of the Philippines on July 4, 1946.

Why June 12 Matters

June 12 is celebrated as Philippine Independence Day. It honors the proclamation of independence and the continuing efforts of Filipinos to build their nation.
