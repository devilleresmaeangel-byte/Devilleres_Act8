People Who Made a Difference

Filipino heroes contributed to our country through writing, leadership, education, and revolutionary action.

José Rizal

1861–1896

José Rizal was a writer and reform advocate. His novels, Noli Me Tangere and El Filibusterismo, criticized abuses under Spanish colonial rule.

Andrés Bonifacio

1863–1897

Andrés Bonifacio was a leading figure in the Katipunan, which organized resistance against Spanish colonial rule.

Emilio Aguinaldo

1869–1964

Emilio Aguinaldo proclaimed Philippine independence on June 12, 1898, and later became president of the First Philippine Republic.

Apolinario Mabini

1864–1903

Apolinario Mabini was a political thinker and adviser who contributed ideas to the revolutionary government.

Remember

Studying different historical sources helps us understand the achievements and challenges of important figures in Philippine history.
