History Across the Centuries

Learn about important events that influenced the development of the Philippines.

Before 1521

Early Communities

Early Filipino communities had their own languages, traditions, leaders, and trading relationships.

1521

Magellan's Expedition

Ferdinand Magellan's expedition reached the Philippine islands, where communities already had established cultures and societies.

1565

Spanish Colonial Rule

Miguel López de Legazpi established a Spanish settlement in Cebu, marking an important step in Spanish colonial rule.

1892

Founding of the Katipunan

Andrés Bonifacio and other patriots helped establish the Katipunan, a secret society that sought independence from Spain.

1896

Philippine Revolution

The revolution against Spanish colonial rule began, leading to widespread resistance.

June 12, 1898

Declaration of Independence

Emilio Aguinaldo proclaimed Philippine independence from Spain in Kawit, Cavite.

July 4, 1946

Independence Recognized

The United States recognized the independence of the Republic of the Philippines.

1986

People Power Revolution

A largely peaceful movement helped end Ferdinand Marcos Sr.'s rule and restore democratic institutions.
